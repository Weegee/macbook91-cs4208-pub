  option prints "macbook91-audio-verify: error: ..." with a "Did you mean:" line where one fits
  and "Help: ... --help", and exits 2. Questions, instructions and errors go to stderr; the check
  lines and the verdict stay on stdout. It asks only on a terminal; without one the listening
  checks are reported SKIPPED. Ctrl+C and SIGTERM print "cancelled" and exit 130 and 143.

  package version into the installed verifier.

  scripts, test runner) have --help and --version and report errors as "PROG: error: ...".
  The collector takes -o/--output and prints only the output directory on stdout.

  CachyOS repositories.
